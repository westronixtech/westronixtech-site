Our Education Mission

WESTRONIX TECH connects quality technology from the United States with educational needs in Togo and across Africa. Our goal is to make computers and digital tools more accessible through responsible reuse, refurbishment, donations and affordable technology programs.

Where We Create Impact

Practical technology programs designed around real educational and community needs.

💻

Computer Access

Reliable refurbished laptops and computers for students, teachers, schools and community programs.

🔬

STEM Learning

Technology that supports science, technology, engineering, mathematics and digital skills.

🏫

Computer Labs

Support for schools and organizations seeking to establish or improve affordable computer labs.

♻️

Responsible Reuse

Give usable technology a second life while helping reduce electronic waste.

How the Program Works

A simple pathway from technology recovery to educational impact.

1

Recover

We identify usable technology through responsible sourcing, IT asset disposition and equipment recovery.

2

Refurbish

Equipment is inspected, tested, prepared for reuse and securely processed before deployment.

3

Connect

Schools, organizations and community partners tell us what technology they need.

4

Impact

Technology reaches learners and educators where it can create lasting digital opportunities.
